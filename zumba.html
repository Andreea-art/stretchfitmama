Why Zumba Works

Burn Calories

Burn between 500-1000 calories per session while having the time of your life.

Full Body Workout

Zumba targets every muscle group through dynamic dance movements.

Stress Relief

Dance away your worries. The music and movement naturally boost your mood.

Make Friends

Join a vibrant community of people who love to move and have fun.

No Experience Needed

Anyone can Zumba! Follow along at your own pace - there's no wrong way to dance.

Online Classes

Zumba From Anywhere

Can't make it to class in person? No problem! I offer live Zoom classes so you can dance with us from the comfort of your home. All you need is a little space and a good internet connection.

Online classes are perfect for busy moms, those with unpredictable schedules, or anyone who prefers to workout at home. You'll still get the full energy and interaction of an in-person class!
