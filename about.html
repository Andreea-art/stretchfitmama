Meet Your Instructor

Andreea Stoian

Certified Zumba Instructor • Fitness Coach • Nutrition Advisor

Hi! I'm Andreea, and I'm passionate about helping women and families discover the joy of movement. I bring the energy and rhythm of Zumba to every class, combined with personalized fitness coaching and practical nutrition advice.

After becoming a mother myself, I realized how challenging it can be for women to prioritize their health and wellbeing. That's why I created Stretch Fit Mama — a community where moms can stay active, connect with other women, and feel supported every step of the way.

Whether you're a beginner taking your first step into fitness, a mom looking to get back in shape, or someone who wants to transform their lifestyle — I'm here to guide you. Let's move with joy!
